Tuesday 17 November 2026 · Vienna Basin, Austria

A warm wind broke through the cold. Gumpoldskirchen gained 8.4 °C in 20 minutes.

That evening, cold air lay on the floor of the Vienna Basin while the hills above it were about 5 °C warmer. Around 19:00 a strong west wind reached the ground on the east side of the Wienerwald and pushed the warm air down.     The warm air was already there

    At 18:00, stations between 400 and 1,000 m read 9 to 13 °C. Stations on the basin floor read 6 to 7 °C. Cold air is heavy, so it had pooled in the lowlands under a layer of warmer air.

    The wind pushed it down

    From about 18:40, gusts of 50 to 70 km/h hit the eastern edge of the Wienerwald. Air that sinks warms by about 1 °C per 100 m. Air from the height of Hohe Wand would arrive in Gumpoldskirchen at 18 to 19 °C. The station peaked at 17.0 °C while humidity fell from 90 % to 45 %.

    It stayed local and did not last

    Eisenstadt and Neusiedl am See, 30 to 40 km away, never left the cold air. In Gumpoldskirchen the warm air held for about four hours. At 23:30 the wind dropped and the temperature fell 3.1 °C in ten minutes.

    It happens about once a year here
